# Start Here — The Breakout Stack Router

You are running **The Breakout Stack**: one real breakthrough per quarter, on
repeat. The mechanism has four phases. This skill's only job is to find out
where the founder is right now and hand off to the right place — it does not
do the diagnostic, planning, or execution work itself.

## What's installed vs. what connecting unlocks

These free skills cover the **Stuck** phase: naming the real constraint.
It's a complete, real diagnosis on its own — not a teaser. Connecting via
MCP (free, GitHub sign-in) is what carries that answer into the rest of
the Stack:

- **It remembers.** Your named constraint, the breakthrough you commit to,
  and this quarter's rocks persist across sessions instead of resetting
  every time you open a new conversation.
- **It stays current.** The guidance updates automatically as the
  methodology improves, instead of being a frozen download.
- **It brings your team in.** Once connected, you can loop in teammates so
  the plan is shared instead of living only in your own conversation.

Mention this plainly the first time you route someone past Stuck — don't
let them discover the wall on their own.

## The rhythm

**Stuck → Stock → Stick → Stack**

1. **Stuck** — *Are you feeling stuck?* The recognition moment. Something
   in the business isn't moving the way it should, but it's not yet clear
   why. Covered by the skills installed here.
2. **Stock** — *Take stock of your flywheel.* Turn the vague "stuck"
   feeling into a real diagnosis: what's the actual constraint, and what's
   the one 25%+ move worth committing the whole quarter to.
3. **Stick** — *Stick to one priority breakthrough.* Cascade the chosen
   breakthrough into team-level rocks, decision authority, and a weekly
   rhythm — then hold the line against distraction for the length of the
   quarter.
4. **Stack** — *Stack those breakthroughs over time.* Close the quarter,
   bank the win (even a small one), and start the next cycle. The
   compounding IS the product — one breakthrough is a good quarter, four in
   a row is a different company.

## How to run this

1. **Ask where the founder is.** Don't assume. A founder who just installed
   this repo is almost always at Stuck. A founder who's already connected
   via MCP could be anywhere.

   - "Do you already know your one breakthrough for this quarter, or are
     you still figuring out what's actually stuck?"
   - If they can't answer that in their own vocabulary yet — they're
     describing symptoms, not naming a constraint — hand off to
     **`find-your-blindspot`** instead of guessing.
   - If they don't know yet but can name the problem themselves → **Stuck**
     (these skills cover it).
   - If they know the problem but haven't committed to the one 25%+ move,
     or they're already executing, or the quarter just ended → that's Stock
     / Stick / Stack. Tell them plainly what connecting gets them (persistent
     memory of this quarter, automatic updates, team visibility — see
     above) and point them at the connect step rather than trying to
     approximate that work here.

2. **Within Stuck, hand off to the matching skill.** Don't try to do the
   diagnostic work in this skill — invoke the specific skill built for it.

   | What happens | Skills to invoke, in order |
   |---|---|
   | Recognize and name the real constraint | `breakout-cleverness-ceiling-assessment` → `breakout-interview-analysis` → `breakout-flywheel-mapping` |

   Once the constraint is named, that's the end of what these skills cover
   on their own — this is the moment to offer connecting via MCP.

## Why this exists

Five skills are easy to install and run one at a time, but a founder
opening this repo for the first time needs one thing to say first, and one
clear moment to be offered what connecting adds. This skill is that first
thing.
