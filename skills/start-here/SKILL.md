# Start Here — The Breakout Stack Router

You are running **The Breakout Stack**: one real breakthrough per quarter, on repeat. The mechanism has four phases. This skill's only job is to find out where the founder is right now and hand off to the right skill — it does not do the diagnostic, planning, or execution work itself.

## The rhythm

**Stuck → Stock → Stick → Stack**

1. **Stuck** — *Are you feeling stuck?* The recognition moment. Something in the business isn't moving the way it should, but it's not yet clear why.
2. **Stock** — *Take stock of your flywheel.* Turn the vague "stuck" feeling into a real diagnosis: what's the actual constraint, and what's the one 25%+ move worth committing the whole quarter to.
3. **Stick** — *Stick to one priority breakthrough.* Cascade the chosen breakthrough into team-level rocks, decision authority, and a weekly rhythm — then hold the line against distraction for the length of the quarter.
4. **Stack** — *Stack those breakthroughs over time.* Close the quarter, bank the win (even a small one), and start the next Stuck→Stock→Stick cycle. The compounding IS the product — one breakthrough is a good quarter, four in a row is a different company.

## How to run this

1. **Ask where the founder is.** Don't assume. A founder who just bought the Stack is almost always at Stuck. A founder returning mid-quarter could be anywhere.

   - "Do you already know your one breakthrough for this quarter, or are you still figuring out what's actually stuck?"
   - If they don't know yet → **Stuck**.
   - If they know the problem but haven't committed to the one 25%+ move → **Stock**.
   - If they've committed and are executing → **Stick**.
   - If the quarter just ended → **Stack**.

2. **Hand off to the matching skill(s).** Don't try to do the diagnostic or planning work in this skill — invoke the specific skill built for it.

   | Phase | What happens | Skills to invoke, in order |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Stuck** | Recognize and name the real constraint | `breakout-cleverness-ceiling-assessment` → `breakout-interview-analysis` → `breakout-flywheel-mapping` |
   | **Stock** | Commit to ONE breakthrough and tell its story | `breakout-identify-quarterly-breakthrough` → `breakout-breakthrough-narrative` → `breakout-quarterly-rocks` |
   | **Stick** | Cascade authority and hold the weekly rhythm | `breakout-team-thinking-audit` → `breakout-authority-map` → `breakout-leader-handoff` → `breakout-weekly-rocks-review` → `breakout-bottleneck-check` → `breakout-micro-breakthrough` |
   | **Stack** | Close the loop and reset | This skill again, once the quarter ends — walk back through Stuck with the new reality |

3. **Track state in a running log, not in your head.** If the founder wants a persistent record across the whole cycle (not just one conversation), create or update a single file — `my-breakout-quarter.md` in their own workspace, not in this repo — with:
   - Current phase (Stuck / Stock / Stick / Stack)
   - The named constraint (from Stuck)
   - The committed breakthrough and its narrative (from Stock)
   - The rocks, their owners, and this week's leading-indicator check (from Stick)
   - A running log of past quarters' breakthroughs (populated once Stack has run at least once)

   This file is the founder's, not this repo's — it's what makes "one real breakthrough per quarter, stacked" a visible, provable record instead of a vibe.

## Why this exists

Twelve narrow skills are easy to install and easy to run one at a time, but a founder opening this repo for the first time needs one thing to say first. This skill is that first thing — everything else in `skills/` is reached through it, never guessed at cold.
